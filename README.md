# BLACK BOOK
A collection of decision-intelligence case studies and relevant market implications.

## PURPOSE
Black Book consolidates individual event-driven decision case studies under a single, organized collection. Each case study explores the thesis, reasoning, and (if available) strategic outcomes of a market or geopolitical event. It is important to note: not all case studies carry market implications and are specifically actionable. The point of Black Book is to showcase decision making under uncertainty and framework rationale.

## FRAMEWORK
Most, if not all, Black Book event case studies are analyzed vis-à-vis the [God's Eye framework](https://github.com/Austin-Angdjaja/gods-eye). By nature, God's Eye is incomplete, and evolves to incorporate new frameworks, rationale, and decision logics.
